Frequently Asked Questions

Is A Felt Sense portable?+
A Felt Sense uses a compact, design-forward hardware unit called a Pillow Bag that sits discreetly in the room, spa, lobby, or elsewhere on the property.
Will this create more work for my front desk staff?+
Minimal. You’ll receive a short onboarding guide, a staff reference card, and access to our team training video. We aim for your front desk to be fully up to speed in 15 minutes.
What setup and maintenance is required?+
All you need to provide is a dedicated space in each room and a power outlet. The pillow bag can be spot cleaned and housekeeping replaces pillow cases between guests.
How is it priced?+
Each pillow bag costs $2,500USD and comes with our basic experience library filled with NSDR meditations and incredible ambient music. For $30/pillow bag/mo you can subscribe to our AI-personalization app that customizes sessions by guest preference and stay context.
Can we start with a pilot?+
Yes, and we encourage it for you and your guests to experience the power of A Felt Sense firsthand.
